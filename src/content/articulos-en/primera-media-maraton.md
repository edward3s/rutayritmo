The half marathon is an honest distance. It does not forgive an improvised plan, but it does not demand the monastic life of a marathon either. With a reasonable base, three or four runs a week and twelve weeks of organized work, you can reach the finish line without crawling.

The starting condition: you can run 5-6 kilometers in a row with relative comfort and you have been moving for at least a couple of months. If that's not you, start with the [8-week plan to start running](/en/diario/empezar-a-correr-en-8-semanas/) and come back in a few months.

> **In short:** three easy runs, one long run and one quality session per week. The long run is not run all out. Race week is about reducing volume, not making up for lost time.

## The 12-week plan

| Week | Long run | Quality | Approximate volume |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 10 km | 3 × 6 min at threshold | 25-30 km |
| 2 | 11 km | 3 × 8 min at threshold | 27-32 km |
| 3 | 12 km | 4 × 6 min at threshold | 30-35 km |
| 4 | 9 km | Easy, no quality | 22-26 km |
| 5 | 13 km | 2 × 12 min at threshold | 32-37 km |
| 6 | 14 km | 4 × 8 min at threshold | 34-39 km |
| 7 | 15 km | 3 × 10 min at threshold | 35-40 km |
| 8 | 11 km | Easy, no quality | 25-30 km |
| 9 | 16 km | 2 × 15 min at threshold | 38-43 km |
| 10 | 18 km | 3 × 8 min at half marathon pace | 40-45 km |
| 11 | 12 km | 2 × 6 min easy | 25-30 km |
| 12 | Race | — | 15-20 km |

Weeks 4 and 8 are down weeks. They are not a punishment: they are the part of the plan where the body absorbs the work. If you reach one of them tired, extend the down week by a couple of days.

## How to run the long run

The long run is run easy, with controlled breathing and the ability to talk. If you finish it out of breath, you have turned an endurance session into a race. The pace can be 30-60 seconds per kilometer slower than your half marathon goal pace.

You don't need to reach 21 kilometers in training. With 18 km as your longest run, that's enough for most recreational runners. The race adds adrenaline, crowds and a pace you shouldn't follow in the first kilometers.

## Race pace

Start slower than you think. The first 3 kilometers should feel easy; if you're already short of breath at kilometer 2, you started too fast. Look for a pace you can hold while speaking in short sentences and save energy for the second half.

A useful reference is [threshold](/en/diario/umbral-sin-laboratorio/) pace: half marathon pace is usually slower, sustainable for 90 minutes. If you haven't done a test, go by effort and by the quality sessions in the plan.

## Race week

- Reduce volume, keep some short intensity so you don't feel flat.
- Prepare your clothes, bib and breakfast the night before.
- Don't wear new shoes or try new gels.
- Sleep as much as you can; the night before is not the one that matters most.
- Arrive early, but not two hours early.

## After the finish line

Walk, drink, eat something and change clothes. Don't sit down suddenly. In the following days, wait 48-72 hours before running easy again. Recovery is not weakness: it is part of training.

If the race goes badly, it doesn't mean the plan was bad. Race day has too many variables. What matters is that you have built a base you can use for the next one. And if you catch the bug, the [first marathon](/en/diario/primera-maraton-plan/) is the next step, but not the only one.
