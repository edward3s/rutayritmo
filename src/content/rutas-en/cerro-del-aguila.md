Cerro del Águila is not a route for breaking in new shoes or for discovering trail running on any given Sunday. It is short, but it packs in rock, roots, hard ramps and a descent that demands attention. If you are coming from the asphalt, be ready to walk more than you would like.

## The route

Start from Navacerrada, on a path that gains height quickly. The first kilometer serves as a warm-up, but soon the trail narrows and the rock appears. There are sections where running is not the fastest option: walking with a firm step saves you energy and ankles.

The climb to the summit has steep gradients and a few steps where it is worth using your hands. It is not climbing, but it is not a park path either. The descent is technical, with loose rock; do not let yourself go even if your legs feel good.

## Profile and pace

The profile is a sustained climb and a fast descent. The highest point is around 1,620 m. A pace of 8:00-9:00 min/km is normal on the runnable sections; total time depends on how much you walk. Do not look at average pace: on trail it does not mean the same as on asphalt.

If this is your first technical route, go with someone who knows it or carry a downloaded track. Navigation in open areas can be tricky in fog.

## Water and fueling

There is no water on the route. Carry 1.5 liters, some food and a warm layer. The weather changes fast in the mountains: it can be sunny at the start and cold and windy on the summit. The [hydration](/en/diario/hidratacion-en-carreras-populares/) guide and the guide on [what to eat before a long run](/en/diario/comer-antes-de-un-rodaje-largo/) help you plan.

## Getting there

Navacerrada is well connected by road. There is parking in the village and near the trailheads, but it fills up on weekends. Arrive early and do not leave the car in prohibited areas: emergency access must stay clear.

## When to go

Spring, early summer and autumn. In winter there can be snow, ice and trail closures; check the conditions before heading out. If there is fog, the route loses its landmarks and gains risk: better another day.

## Safety

It is a demanding route for everyday runners. Do not do it alone if you have no mountain experience. Carry a charged phone, tell someone your schedule and check the forecast. Respect the environment: do not leave the marked paths and do not leave trash.

If you have knee or ankle issues, technical terrain is not the best place to test them. Check the [knee pain](/en/diario/dolor-de-rodilla-cuando-parar/) guide and, if the pain persists, get advice before going back.
